### Devin Phillip Davis

Independent researcher, Agile Defense Systems LLC. I work on governed, local-first AI tooling and
on small, pre-registered experiments in quantum control and artificial life.

**Working practice.** Each experiment states its pass criterion before it runs. Results are
recorded as PASS or FAIL, including negative results, in a scorecard generated from the raw
artifacts. Software releases are archived on Zenodo with DOIs.
